# Ambient music

Put a track you are licensed to use here as `wizarding-ambience.mp3` (the path is `assets.music` in
`src/lib/assets.ts`). A calm instrumental, 2 to 4 minutes, loops seamlessly; keep it under about 5 MB.

Do not use the Harry Potter film score or any commercial recording without a licence. Royalty-free or
Creative Commons libraries are the usual route; read the licence of the exact track, and add the credit
it requires (for CC BY tracks, a line in the footer or this README). Until the file exists the music box
shows that no file was found and its controls are disabled.

Playback never starts by itself: the visitor presses play. If they left it playing last time, it resumes
on their first tap or key press.
